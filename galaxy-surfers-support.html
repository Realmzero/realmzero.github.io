Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Swipe left or right to change lanes, swipe up to jump, swipe down to slide. Double-tap the shield button (or the screen) to raise your shield once it's charged. Collect orbs to score, keep a perfect streak to charge your shield, and grab coins to unlock new characters in the shop.

I lost my coins or characters after reinstalling

Progress is restored from iCloud. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple ID and that iCloud Drive is on (iOS Settings ▸ your name ▸ iCloud). Then open the game and wait on the main menu for about 30 seconds.

The "Free Revive" / "Double your coins" button doesn't appear

Those buttons only show when an ad is available. If no ad is ready (for example, without an internet connection), the buttons are hidden and the game continues normally.

My score isn't on the leaderboard

Sign in to Game Center in iOS Settings ▸ Game Center, then play a run. Your best score is sent after each game over.

How do I change the language?

Tap the gear on the main menu and choose your language. The game supports English, Arabic, Spanish, Portuguese, French, German, Italian, Russian, Turkish, Indonesian, Japanese and Korean.

Haptics / sound / music

All three can be switched on or off from the gear on the main menu.
